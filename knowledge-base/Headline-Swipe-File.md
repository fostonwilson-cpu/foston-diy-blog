# Headline Swipe File

Patterns for the `<h2>` category title and the one-sentence `<p>` framing beneath it. Pick the closest pattern, don't invent a new voice from scratch.

## Category title (`<h2>`) patterns

- **Domain & Function** — `"{Noun} {Noun}"`, e.g. "End-User Support", "Security Operations", "Device Management". Two words, no punctuation, title case. This is the dominant pattern on the page — default to it.
- **Domain "&" Domain** — for categories spanning two closely related concerns, e.g. "Collaboration & Productivity", "Governance & Compliance". Use the ampersand entity `&amp;` in the HTML.

Avoid: questions, gerunds ("Managing Devices"), or anything longer than three words.

## Framing sentence (`<p>`) patterns

One sentence, no period-less fragments, verb-first outcome framing:

- `"{Verb} {outcome} with {method}."` — e.g. "Keep inboxes calm with scripts for the most frequent requests." / "Respond quickly to threats with actionable playbooks."
- `"{Verb} {outcome} without {tradeoff}."` — e.g. "Stay audit-ready without sacrificing agility."
- `"{Verb} {domain object} {qualifier}."` — e.g. "Keep networks and workloads reliable with proven runbooks."

The sentence should name the payoff (calm inboxes, fast response, audit-readiness), not the activity ("This section covers ticket procedures").

## Bullet phrasing patterns

- `"{Deliverable noun phrase} for/covering {scope}"` — e.g. "Printer queue reset checklist for Windows and macOS", "Email sync repair guide covering Outlook, Gmail, and mobile apps."
- `"{Deliverable noun phrase} aligned to {standard}"` — e.g. "Ransomware containment checklist aligned to NIST guidance."
- Lead with the deliverable type (checklist, playbook, matrix, template, guide, kit), not the underlying problem.
